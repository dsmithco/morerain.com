The Vision

More Rain is acoustic worship music that's free for anyone to use.

The idea behind More Rain is simple: to give people music that helps them engage with God. There's no paywall. They're acoustic worship songs that anyone can pick up and sing.

Every recording is shared under a Creative Commons license, so you can listen, download, and use these songs however you want.
