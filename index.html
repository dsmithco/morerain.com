The Vision

More Rain is free acoustic worship music — written simply, given freely.

The heart behind More Rain is plain: to give music to people to help them engage with God in some way. No paywall, no polish for its own sake — just honest acoustic songs of worship that anyone can pick up, sing, and make their own.

Every recording is shared under a Creative Commons license, so you're free to listen, download, play, and use these songs in your gatherings and quiet places alike.
